Purpose

Why Support Side exists

Local businesses get stuck with software that doesn’t fit, or with prices that don’t make sense. They need tools that match how they actually work: booking, scheduling, inventory, customer intake, or a site customers can use, without an agency markup or a bloated subscription.

That’s what Support Side does. I build safe, affordable software of any kind to support the business. Right now that work is a booking website for a local rental LLC. Next time it might be something else. The job is the same: listen, build what they need, keep it maintainable.

The owner stays on the actual work. I stay on the support side.
