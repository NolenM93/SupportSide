Last updated: October 2026

Support Side Tech is a web and software development business operated by Nolen Millington in Dunedin, Florida. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit supportsidetech.com or contact us through the site.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you use our contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any message or details you include in the form

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide through the contact form to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Discuss potential projects or services
  • Follow up on conversations you start

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information stays between us.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site. This helps us improve the experience and see which pages are most helpful.

We may use third-party analytics services (like Google Analytics) that collect anonymous data about page visits, browser type, and general location (city or region level, not your specific address). These tools help us understand traffic patterns but do not identify you personally.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer not to be tracked.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. Form submissions are processed through secure, reputable services. We do not store sensitive payment information on this website, as we do not process payments directly through the site.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form uses Formspree to process submissions. When you submit the form, your information passes through their service. You can review Formspree's privacy policy for details on how they handle data.

Your Rights

If you want to know what information we have about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete your data, email us at nolenmillington@supportsidetech.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page occasionally.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, contact us at nolenmillington@supportsidetech.com.
