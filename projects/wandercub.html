Mobile App · Personal Project

WanderCub

A family vacation app that serves short "micro-adventures" for kids while you're on the road. Turn the drive into part of the trip.

Personal Project Mobile

The Idea

Long car rides with kids are hard. "Are we there yet?" starts twenty minutes in. WanderCub is built around a simple premise: what if the journey could be as fun as the destination?

The app delivers location-aware "micro-adventures"—short activities, games, and challenges that match what's passing outside the window. Spot a red barn. Count license plates from three different states. Find something blue at the next rest stop.

Features

Location-Aware

Activities that make sense for where you actually are.

Rewards & Badges

Kids collect achievements for completed adventures.

Family-Friendly

Activities designed for kids of different ages.

Offline Mode

Works without cell service in remote areas.

Tech Stack

Dart Flutter Firebase Google Maps API

Status

WanderCub is a personal project in development. The codebase is currently private while the core features are being built. Once the app reaches a testable state, I plan to release it on iOS and Android.
