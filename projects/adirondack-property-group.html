Client Project · In Progress

Adirondack Property Group

A booking website for a local rental company in the Adirondacks. Real availability, real reservations, and a flow that customers can actually use—without calling a phone number or guessing at dates.

In Development Private Client

The Problem

A local property rental business in the Adirondack region needed a way for customers to book rentals online. The existing process—phone calls, emails, back-and-forth about dates—wasn't scaling. Customers wanted to see what was available and book it themselves.

The owner needed something straightforward: show the properties, show availability, take the reservation. No agency markup, no bloated SaaS subscription that charges per booking.

The Solution

A custom-built booking website that shows real-time availability, handles reservations, and lets the owner manage everything from a simple dashboard. The customer picks dates, sees what's open, and books. The owner gets notified and can confirm or adjust.

Availability Calendar

Real dates, real availability. No guessing, no phone tag.

Online Booking

Customers complete the entire reservation process online.

Owner Dashboard

Manage properties, view bookings, adjust availability.

Mobile-First

Works on any device—phones, tablets, desktops.

Tech Stack

TypeScript Next.js React Tailwind CSS

Status
