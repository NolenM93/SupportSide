Lantern Hollow

Best on a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse. Click once to start sound, then click the title card to play.

A playable 3D wave-defense game in the browser. You hold the last lantern in a cartoon graveyard, pop goofy zombies, build candy-powered traps, and spend coins on Hollow Street between rounds.

Playable Open Source
Play game → View on GitHub

What It Does

Lantern Hollow is a kid-friendly spooky game, not a horror title. Rounds of cartoon zombies come through the graveyard. You shoot, swing a bat, and drop traps that run on candy. Between waves you walk Hollow Street and spend what you earned.

There is no login. Open the page, click once so the browser allows sound, then click again on the title card when you are ready. A short help card lists the controls. Press H any time to read it again.

How to Play

Move and look

WASD to walk, mouse to look around. F toggles first-person. Scroll zooms. Esc pauses.

Defend the lantern

Left click shoots. V swings the bat. R reloads. Enter calls the next wave.

Build traps

Tab opens the trap builder. Keys 1 to 9 pick the hotbar. Candy powers the defenses you drop.

Hollow Street

Between rounds, press E to buy, open inventory, or use town machines with the coins you collected.
