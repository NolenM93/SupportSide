Web App · Spatial Computing

Stanza

A spatial decision log for furniture arrangement. Upload a photograph, get a measured 3D model, rearrange the pieces, and keep a record of every layout you tried and why you rejected it.

Live Demo Open Source

What It Does

Most photo-to-3D tools stop at the picture. The picture is the easy half. The hard half is the decision—which arrangement you actually commit to, and remembering next spring why you moved the bookcase back.

Stanza turns an ordinary room photograph into a measured 3D model, lets you rearrange the furniture that's already in the room, and keeps a log of every arrangement you tried. It doesn't try to sell you a sofa. It tells you whether the one you have will fit.

Key Features

Photo to 3D

Camera calibration from vanishing points. Real dimensions in metres.

Constraint Engine

Door swings, walking routes, outlet reach, viewing distances.

Daylight Simulation

Solar position projected through windows for any date and hour.

Move Planner

Numbered instructions: what moves, in what order, how many people.

How It Works

  1. 1 Upload a photograph. A Web Worker runs line detection and vanishing point estimation to calibrate the camera.
  2. 2 Mark the floor and furniture. Each 2D box is lifted into a metric 3D object with real dimensions.
  3. 3 Drag pieces around. The constraint engine checks door clearances, walking routes, and ergonomic distances.
  4. 4 Commit or reject. Every arrangement is logged with your rationale. Print the move plan and hand it over.
