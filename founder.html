Support Side Tech is a freelance web and software studio in Dunedin, Florida, run by Nolen Millington. Nolen founded the studio to build software that matches how local businesses actually work. We stay on the support side so owners can stay on the actual work.

Based in Dunedin, Florida, and working with clients in the Tampa Bay area and beyond. Outside the studio: fishing, hiking, family, and making things with other people.

How we work

A studio, not an agency markup

We talk to the people who run the work, scope a real first version, and write the code ourselves. No account manager in the middle. No bloated subscription that charges per booking.

That can be a website, SEO and Google Business Profile support, hosting, or a custom tool. The job is the same: listen, build what they need, keep it maintainable.
