Questions about hosting

What is website hosting?

Hosting is where your website lives on the internet. Without it, your site goes offline and nobody can visit it. These plans include that hosting, plus the care that keeps the site running.

What happens if I don't have a plan?

You can skip a plan. You would then handle hosting, updates, and backups yourself, or find someone else when you need them. If the site goes down or needs a change, no one is already looking after it. We can still help as one-off work when you ask.

What counts as a "small edit"?

Updating text, swapping images, adjusting business hours, adding a new team member, or fixing a typo. Bigger changes like new pages or features are quoted separately.

Do I need a hosting plan if you built my site?

Not required, but recommended. Every site needs a place to live, plus updates over time. A hosting plan means you do not have to think about it.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What if I need more than the monthly edit hours?

Additional hours are billed at an hourly rate we agree on upfront. No surprises.
