Questions about plans

What counts as a "small edit"?

Updating text, swapping images, adjusting business hours, adding a new team member, or fixing a typo. Bigger changes like new pages or features are quoted separately.

Do I need a care plan if you built my site?

Not required, but recommended. Sites need updates and attention over time. A care plan means you do not have to think about it.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

What if I need more than the monthly edit hours?

Additional hours are billed at an hourly rate we agree on upfront. No surprises.
